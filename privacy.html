Privacy policy

Last changed September 23, 2026

Trophy Case is an app for saving your achievements and getting advice on them. It is a small independent project, and it was built so that it knows as little about you as possible. This page says exactly what it keeps, where, and who else sees anything.

The short version

  • Your achievements and photos stay on your own phone or computer. We never have a copy.
  • If you sign in, our server keeps your email address and a few details needed to run your account. Nothing about your achievements.
  • The AI only sees something when you ask it to. Then it goes to Anthropic, the company that makes the AI, to get the answer.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We never sell or share your information.
  • Accounts are for people 13 and older.

What stays on your device

Every achievement, photo, goal, ranking, profile summary and Scout conversation is stored in your browser's own storage on the device you are using. It is not sent to our server and there is no cloud copy. That also means that if you delete the app's data or lose the device without a backup, it is gone. Settings has "Back up everything", which makes a file you keep wherever you like. We never see that file.

Phone photos usually contain hidden details such as where and when they were taken. The app removes those details from every photo when you save it.

The Me tab's "Save as image" draws the picture on your device. Nothing is uploaded to make it; it goes wherever you choose to share or save it.

If you tell the app your first name so it can say hi, that name is kept on your device only. It is never sent to the AI or to our server, and it is not in backups.

What our server keeps if you sign in

You only need an account for the AI features. If you make one, the server stores exactly this:

  • a random id for your account
  • your email address, as confirmed by Google
  • your plan (every account is on the free plan today)
  • when you made the account
  • sign-in codes that keep you signed in, which expire after 90 days
  • the times you used the AI in the last 5 hours, which delete themselves. They are counted only to stop anyone abusing the service.

That is the whole list. No achievements, no photos, no goal, no conversations. Our host, Vercel, keeps ordinary short-lived server logs (such as the time of a request and the address it came from), like every website.

Who else sees anything

Anthropic, for the AI

When you use an AI feature, the app sends what that feature needs to Anthropic through our server, and nothing is ever sent in the background:

  • reading a photo: a smaller copy of that one photo, with its hidden details already removed, and the names of your categories so it can pick one
  • ranking, next steps, your profile summary and Scout: your goal and the words of your achievements, never their photos
  • Scout: your recent messages, the names of your categories, and a file only if you attach one to a message

Our server passes it on and stores none of it. Anthropic processes it to write the answer. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, what is sent through its API is not used to train its AI.

Google, for signing in

"Continue with Google" means Google knows you signed in to Trophy Case, and tells us your email address. We keep only the email. Nothing about your achievements goes to Google.

Age

Accounts are for people 13 and older. Before signing in, the app asks for a birth month and year. It uses the answer once to check the age and then forgets it: the date is never saved or sent. Anyone can use the rest of the app without an account. If you are a parent and think your child under 13 has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Deleting your information

  • Your account: Settings, then "Delete my account". It is removed from our server straight away.
  • Everything on your device: Settings, then "Delete everything".
  • Or email us and we will delete your account for you. We cannot reach anything on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If we ever start keeping something new, this page will say so before it happens.
